BASIC GEOMETRY
CONCEPTS
Angle: When two non-parallel and co-planar lines
(lines in the same plane) intersect, at the point of
intersection the measure of rotational displacement is
called an angle.
Types of Angles: If θ is an angle such that 
1) If θ = 0o then θ is zero angle.
2) If 0o < θ < 90o then θ is called an acute angle.
3) If θ = 90o then θ is right angle.
4) If θ > 900 then θ is obtuse angle.
5) If θ = 180o then θ is called a straight angle.
6) If 180o < θ < 360o then θ  is called reflex angle.
7) If θ = 360o  then θ is called complete angle.
Parallel and Non-Parallel lines:
1) Two lines are said to be parallel lines if they are co-
planar (in the same plane) and non intersecting. 
The point of intersection of parallel lines is at infinite
places which is not real.
2) The angle between parallel lines is undefined, or it
can be either 0o or 180o or any multiple of 180o. 
3) Two lines are said to be non parallel (inclined lines)
if they are co-planar and intersect at a real point.
The point of intersection of inclined lines is real.
