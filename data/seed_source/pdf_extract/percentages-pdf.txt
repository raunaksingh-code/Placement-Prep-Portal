CONCEPTS
A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a
fraction of 100. The word 'per cent' or 'percentage'
means for every one hundred. In other words, it gives
rate of a parameter per hundred. It is denoted by the
symbol %.
e.g.: 30% means 30 out of one hundred or 30
